Descanto is opening early access: persistent cloud computers for AI agents. Desktops hibernate when idle and resume exactly where they left off — tabs, processes, and logins intact — in about half a second.

Fork a running machine twenty ways for parallel runs at ~225ms per clone. Billing runs only while your desktop responds, and overage is strictly opt-in.
