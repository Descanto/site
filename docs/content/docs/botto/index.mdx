---
title: What is Botto
description: The open, local-first AI teammate for your Mac.
---

Botto is an open-source, local-first "AI teammates" platform. It runs as a
Bun daemon (`bot-server`) on your own machine, and owns Bot identity,
conversations, durable harness sessions, memory, routines, plugins, backups,
and a WebSocket API -- so your Bots keep working, and keep their state, on
hardware you control.

## Where to find it

- **GitHub**: [descanto/bot](https://github.com/descanto) -- the source of
  truth, MIT-licensed, open to contributions.
- **Web**: [botto.descanto.com](https://botto.descanto.com) -- download the
  app or read about what Botto can do.
